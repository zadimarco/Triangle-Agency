At the beginning of a mission, think about your future and pick either **Anomaly**, **Reality**, or **Competency** and roll **Dynamism**, **Empathy**, or **Professionalism**.

## On a Success,
You grow up. Look at the playwalled document that is next available on the track you picked. Anomaly abilities are considered to be on the Anomaly track. You immediately resolve that playwalled document. You have access to everything that playwalled document entails until the next mission. If you despise that future, you may take 3 harm as you rip at the strands of time. It has not come to pass... yet.

## On a Triscendence,
Every member of the team may also resolve the success effect.

## On a failure,
Knowledge of that future makes it unattainable. You may look at the document and all immediately attached playwall documents, but it is locked to you. This ability may not be used again. Gain the following track.

When you make a decision that cannot be undone, mark this track:
- [ ] 1
- [ ] 2
- [ ] Remove the lock on the playwalled document and the ability to use "My Future" again
