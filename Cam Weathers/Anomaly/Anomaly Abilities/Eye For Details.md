When something isn't quite right, point it out and roll **Attentiveness**.
## On a success,
Edit it out of the picture. A mundane object, person, or minor anomaly you pointed at is gone for as long as you continue to stare where they once were. No one can tell anything is missing from that space
## Spend additional 3s on the following
- You may instead concentrate on a mental image
- The person's memories mend the gap in time
- The object stays gone for an additional minute after you stop looking
- You gain a surface level understanding of whatever you made disappear
## On a Failure,
You can't stop yourself from noticing every little detail out of place around you. For the rest of the mission, you receive additional burnout on any roll you make with your eyes open.
