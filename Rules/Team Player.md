New rule added by Cam

Any agent who has not participated in a **contested roll** since the conclusion of the previous mission may choose a single **Team Player Modifier** they completed the requirement for during the mission. They may choose if this modified superlative counts as the base superlative for all other effects. The Agency may not control how a Team Player spends their time.

These modifiers are:
- Sanctioned: Completed all sanctioned behaviors.
	- Gain a QA a previous character you played had and 3 commendations after superlatives are assigned.
- People Pleasing: Didn't cause a mundane person's death.
	- Gain a relationship a previous character you played had at 3 connection and erase 3 loose ends.
- Anomalous: Produced 15 chaos
	- Gain an anomaly ability a previous character you played had and 7 demerits after superlatives are assigned.
