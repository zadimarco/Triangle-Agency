You create and can now wield a powerful artifact: Big Hand. Big Hand is a long, elegant, sword-like weapon that deals no Harm. It can be activated by hitting a target or by tapping it against any clock. When you wield Big Hand alone, roll **Dynamism**.

## On a success, 
the target is hit with Big Hand. They experience the last thing that Harmed them again, receiving an equal amount of Harm. Tapped clocks ignore this effect.
## On Triscendence,
you can experience the next 60 seconds in any order. You may bring any objects or people
you can hold with you as you travel between seconds in this way.
## For each additional 3, you may choose one of the following:
- Time advances by one minute.
- Time rewinds by one minute.
- A nearby target hears a distracting “tick tock” sound in their mind that prevents them from focusing on any complex plan, math, or speech for the next minute. A nearby timer advances to its end and the task it was applied to completes. (The toast pops out, a parking meter hits its limit, etc.)
## On a failure, 
the next thing that Harms you does so twice, for double the Harm.
